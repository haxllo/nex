## It tells you what just changed

Nex used to update silently and leave you guessing. Now, the first time you open the update button after an update, you get a What's New view with the highlights of the release — plus a Good to know box with the hidden gestures most people never find.

## What changed

- **What's New view**: After an update, the update button turns into a "What's new" entry (once per version). Opening it shows what changed in plain words, fetched from the release notes. Dismiss it and everything goes back to normal.
- **Media volume control**: The media view (Tab while music plays) now has a volume slider and mute button in the transport row, styled to match. Arrow keys nudge it while the view is open.
- **Media view stopped dying on odd sources**: Sessions from random apps or closed tabs used to freeze and crash the overlay. Dead sessions are now skipped, the view only opens for live playback, and a stuck read can't pile up background work anymore.

### Bug Fixes

- Pressing Tab while a random non-Spotify source played could freeze and crash the overlay.
- Seeking on live streams or unsupported sources no longer hangs.
