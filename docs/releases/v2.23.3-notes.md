## No more flash-close or half-painted opens

Shows no longer hide themselves on late focus flaps, and the window appears with content instead of the empty search box first.

**GitHub release title:** exactly `v2.23.3` (version only, no descriptive suffix - the descriptive title lives in this body heading).

## What changed

- **Deferred hide everywhere**: Focus loss past the settle window re-checks live focus before dismissing, so background load and notifications stop killing the overlay.
- **Reveal on first content**: The window holds hidden until results paint (500ms cap) instead of flashing the idle state.

### Bug Fixes

- **Flash-then-auto-close**: Late focus events after idle or load hid the overlay instantly.
- **Search-box-alone opens**: First paint showed the empty window before results landed.
