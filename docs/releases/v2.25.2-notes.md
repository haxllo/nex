## A quieter update button

The Update pill no longer narrates its own progress — clicking it disables quietly against double-launch, progress lives in the status line, and success hides the pill outright. Only failures resurface it as Retry. Pill icons are optically centered, too.

## What changed

### Bug Fixes

- **Update pill chatter removed**: no more "Updating…" → lingering "Up to date" dance next to What's New.
- **Pill icon alignment**: glyph and label share a true center in both pills.
