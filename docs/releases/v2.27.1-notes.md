## Glass that is live from the first frame

A short polish pass on the Liquid Glass panel: opening it over playing video no longer shows a held picture, the surface is lighter with quieter grain, and text on a bright backdrop lost its shadow.

## What changed

- **Opening over playing video starts live**: the panel could appear and then sit on one frozen frame for a moment before snapping into motion — most obvious with a video or animation running behind it. It now shows what is happening behind it from the moment it appears. Three separate causes sat in the way, and the last one was the real one: the opening "settle" of the glass (`swell`) was still re-painting the held picture every frame while the fresh captures underneath it were being thrown away. It now asks the right question — how long ago the desktop last handed us a frame, not whether the previous pass happened to get one — so a pause in the desktop still animates the settle off the held frame, and anything moving drives it with live frames.
- **Live frames are used the moment the panel is revealed**: the reveal used to be carried by the picture the hidden poll had taken, up to a quarter of a second earlier. A show now pulls the newest desktop frame into the mirror first, so the first thing seen is current even before a new frame arrives.
- **Lighter glass, quieter grain**: the wash over the backdrop is lighter and the fine speckle that hides banding is quieter, so the panel reads as clean glass over what is behind it rather than a milky layer on top.
- **No shadow under the text on a light panel**: a dark backdrop keeps the soft shadow under the text — that is what keeps it readable over a video. On a light backdrop the same shadow read as a smudge under the letters, so it is gone there.

### Notes

- Nothing changed about what the glass can show: content Windows blocks from being copied (some protected video) still comes through as a flat area, an HDR monitor still gets a normal-range copy of the screen, and a panel pushed against a monitor edge can still hold its last frame briefly.
- The opening settle of the lens is unchanged at about a fifth of a second. It is now carried by the live picture instead of the frozen one, so it reads as motion rather than a stuck image.
