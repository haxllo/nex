## Glass that comes back

Switching the panel between liquid glass and the solid black/white background
used to leave a black, square-cornered panel until you restarted Nex. Glass now
returns as soon as you pick it again, and it also comes back on its own after a
game or a display change interrupted it.

## What changed

- **Glass survives a background switch**: pick liquid glass, switch to white or
  black, then back — the rounded, blurred panel returns immediately instead of a
  flat black rectangle with sharp corners.
- **Glass recovers by itself**: when a fullscreen game, a monitor change or a
  driver hiccup cut Nex off from the desktop, Nex used to give up on glass for
  the rest of the session. It now notices when the picture is available again
  and turns glass back on without a restart.

## Bug Fixes

- **Black, square panel until restart**: the native glass layer was put to sleep
  for the opaque panel modes and never woken up when glass was chosen again. It
  now wakes on the same switch, with the rounded edge restored.
- **Glass stuck off after capture was lost**: a transient desktop-capture
  failure latched the opaque fallback for the life of the process, even though
  capturing had recovered a second later. The fallback is now cleared on the
  first good frame.

## Notes

- The plugin and README changes in this release are development-side; nothing
  about the overlay behaves differently because of them.
