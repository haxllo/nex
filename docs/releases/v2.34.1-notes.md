## Glass opens cleanly on every show

Liquid Glass no longer flashes a dark panel when the overlay appears. The
Appearance setting now uses the familiar Dark and Light labels for solid
themes.

## What changed

- **Liquid Glass show reliability**: the overlay waits for the current glass
  frame and matching page surface before appearing. If glass is delayed, the
  overlay still appears promptly with its opaque fallback.
- **Clearer appearance names**: the settings control is called Appearance and
  offers Dark, Light, and Liquid Glass.

## Bug Fixes

- **Dark flash before glass appears**: repeated shows on a static desktop could
  miss the acknowledgement for a valid held glass frame. The frame now
  completes the reveal handshake, keeping glass visible across show cycles.
