## Quick Launch keeps every pinned app within reach

Nex no longer limits the apps you pin. Compact view now puts your pinned apps beside search as launch icons, sized like result icons, and hides the power menu.

## What changed

- **No pin cap**: Auto-Fill Limit now controls only automatically suggested apps; every app you pin remains available.
- **Compact app shortcuts**: launch pinned apps from icons beside search. The icons match the size of result icons, and compact view hides the power menu.

### Features

- **Unlimited Quick Launch pins**: pin as many apps as you want.
- **Launch from compact search**: use pinned app icons beside the search box.
