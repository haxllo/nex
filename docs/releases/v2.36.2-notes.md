## Two-bar polish after the pill states

Follow-up on v2.36.1: the compact bar stops clipping pinned apps when both
action pills show, and the What's new page footer is properly sized.

## What changed

### Bug Fixes

- **Pins clipped when Update + What's new show together**: with both pills
  visible the pinned row could cut icons in half → the row keeps its width
  and the search input yields the space instead. (Single What's new pill was
  never affected.) Pill appearance also re-checks the row sizing directly,
  since no resize event fires on a pill toggle.
- **Oversized What's new footer**: the "Got it" button and its footer bar
  were chunkier than everything else on the page → both tightened to match
  the bar's density.
